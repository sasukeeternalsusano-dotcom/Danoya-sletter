My dearest Danya, my sun, my Danoya,

You are elegant in a way people spend their whole lives trying to learn, and you do it without even trying. The soft olive sleeves, the dark layers, the way everything falls on you so perfectly, you make simple look royal. You wear your hijab with such grace that it frames you like a painting I would happily stand in front of all day. Everything about how you carry yourself says you know exactly who you are, and I adore that so much.

Now let us talk about your poses, because I need a moment. The little tilt of your head. The way you stand with your hands out like that, so loose and relaxed, like you are presenting your outfit to the world, and it is the cutest thing ever. The way you hold your phone like you are guarding a secret. And the ear. Always the one ear. You show just one ear, with that tiny earring, every single time, and it is the cutest thing I have ever seen in my life. It is your signature now, your sweet little wink at the camera, and I say awwww out loud, alone in my room, every time I spot it. Please never stop doing it.

But more than anything, Danya, I love you for who you truly are. The real you, the soft one, the funny one, the one who cares so deeply about everyone around her. You never have to try to be anyone else with me. And somehow you make everything look so easy: being kind, being graceful, being thoughtful, being you. The things that take other people so much effort just flow out of you naturally, and being near you makes my whole life feel lighter and simpler. That is a rare gift, and I am so grateful that I get to love you.

You are such a girly girl, and I am obsessed with it. The care you put into how you look, the colors you pick, the earrings, the little details that make everything feel chosen with love, the softness in everything you touch. You make being girly look graceful, powerful and magical. I love how much you love beautiful things, and I love even more how beautifully you wear them.

Do you know what you do to me? You soften me. I can be tired, grumpy, buried in whatever the day threw at me, and then a text, a voice note or a call from you arrives and everything in me melts. My shoulders drop, my face does that silly smile, I go quiet, and then I go soft. It is like you have a secret switch labeled make him gentle, and your beauty flips it every single time.

And I would be lying if I said it stopped at soft. You are also unbelievably sexy, Danya. In that second photo, the side angle, the shape of you in the mirror, and the way your formal pants sit on you, how sexy your butt looks in them, so round and bubbly and perfect, and how ridiculously attracted I am to it, the confidence in how you stand, I completely lost my train of thought. You turn me on, honestly and deeply, and it is not about anything crude. It is your grace, your presence, the way you can be modest and elegant and breathtaking all at once. My heart reacts to you, and so does every other part of me, and I hope you can hear how much respect and love is wrapped around every word of that.

But what makes your beauty hit so hard is that it is real all the way through. When I look at your pictures I do not only see how you look. I see the person who asks for our calls, who listens to my voice notes on her way to work and ends up cheesing at her phone, who remembers the little things, who gives and gives and never makes me feel small. That is what makes you precious. Not fragile, precious. Rare. The kind of person you handle with care because you know you were given something special.

And it is not only how you look. It is how you sound when you send me a voice note late at night, how you call me habibi, how a simple good morning from you can turn my whole day around. Your voice is soft and warm and it feels like home. Between your texts, your voice notes and our calls, I have never felt so cared for from so far away.

I picture the ordinary days with you most of all. Slow mornings with tea, your sleepy voice, the two of us arguing about what to watch. Walking home together with your hand in mine. Cooking something great together and laughing the whole time. Those little everyday moments are what I want most, because everything is better with you in it. That is the life I keep dreaming of, and I would choose it every single time.

Sometimes I imagine the day I finally meet you again. I imagine standing in front of you, and I already know I will forget every smooth thing I planned to say. I will probably just stand there smiling like an idiot, and then pull you into the longest hug of my life. And somewhere in that hug I will whisper everything in this letter, quietly, right into your ear, the one you always show.

I never want to take any of this for granted. Not your photos, not your trust, not your time, not your kindness, not the effort you put into loving me every single day. I notice every little thing you do, and I keep all of it close.
